How To Use PolyEdge

What is PolyEdge?

PolyEdge tracks skilled Polymarket traders and alerts you when they place bets - before the odds shift. By copying experienced traders early, you can get better prices than if you waited.

Finding Good Traders to Follow

  • Look for traders with ROI above +10% - this means they're consistently profitable
  • Win rate above 55% means they win more than they lose
  • Minimum 20+ trades so the stats are meaningful, not luck
  • Check their history - do they specialise in one category (crypto, sports, politics)?

Using Copy Alerts

  • Set Min $ to filter out small noise trades - try $50 or $100 as a starting point
  • The trader's ROI badge is shown on each alert - green means profitable
  • Click the trader name to see their full history
  • Speed matters - copy early before the odds move against you

Consensus Signals

  • When multiple wallets bet the same side on the same market, that's a stronger signal
  • The more wallets and the higher the combined $, the more conviction behind the move
  • Quality wallets (green tags) = traders with positive ROI - weight these more heavily
  • Set Min wallets to 3+ or 5+ to filter only the strongest signals

Telegram Alerts

Set up Telegram in the Saved Traders tab to get instant notifications - even when this app is closed. This is the fastest way to act on a copy alert before the market moves.
